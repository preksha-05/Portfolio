# Preksha Anand · Portfolio

**[preksha-05.github.io](https://preksha-05.github.io/)**

A hand-built, binary-themed portfolio. Everything on the page is stored as bits until you look at it.

## Things to try

- **Disturb the name.** "Preksha Anand" is drawn with live zeros and ones. Move over it to scatter the bits; click to send a shockwave that flips them.
- **Watch the work decode.** Each project cover arrives as bits and decodes as you scroll to it. Click a cover to flip it back.
- **Make it rain.** Press **rain 01** in the header (or type `rain` in the shell). Move your cursor through it and the rain parts around you.
- **Talk to the shell.** Press `/` anywhere. Try `help`, `ls`, `open nestlink`, `binary hello`, `decode 01101000 01101001`, `rain` and `sudo hire preksha`.
